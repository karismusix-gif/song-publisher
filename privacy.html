Privacy policy

Song Publisher — last updated 2026

Song Publisher is a private application that runs on the author's own home server. It is used by one person — the owner of the YouTube channel it publishes to — and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): used only to upload a video file the owner has reviewed and explicitly published.
  • YouTube account management (youtube): used only to set the thumbnail of the video it just uploaded and, if the owner chooses, add that video to a playlist on the same channel.

No other Google data is requested. The application does not read other channels, viewers, comments, analytics, subscriptions or messages, and it cannot delete anything on the account.

What is stored, and where

  • An OAuth refresh token, stored in a file readable only by the application on the owner's own server. It is used to upload without asking for sign-in again.
  • The song files, rendered videos and a log of uploaded video identifiers — all on the owner's own local storage.

What is shared

Nothing. No data is sent to any third party, no analytics or telemetry is collected, and no data is sold, transferred or used for advertising or for training models. The only outbound traffic is the video upload itself to Google's YouTube Data API.

Retention and removal

Files stay on the owner's server until the owner deletes them. Access to the Google account can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token.
