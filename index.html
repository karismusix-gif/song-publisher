Song Publisher

A private, self-hosted tool for publishing my own music to my own YouTube channel.

Song Publisher is a small application that runs on my home server. It takes song files I have already recorded or generated myself, lets me trim and fade them, renders a simple video with a still background image, and uploads that video to the YouTube channel I own.

What it does

  • Shows a waveform of a local audio file so I can set trim points and fades.
  • Renders the audio into a video file on my own hardware.
  • Uploads the finished video to my own YouTube channel with a title, description and tags I enter.
  • Lists which of my songs have already been published.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of the channel. There are no other users, no accounts to create, and the application is not distributed or offered as a service to anyone else.

Google account access

It asks for permission to upload videos to my channel, set video thumbnails and add videos to my playlists. Nothing else is requested, and no Google data is read, stored or shared beyond the video identifiers it needs to link back to what it uploaded. Details are in the privacy policy.
